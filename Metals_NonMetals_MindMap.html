Model answer (practice, 4 marks)

Metals are usually solid, whereas non-metals are usually gases or liquids. Metals are shiny, malleable, ductile and sonorous, but non-metals are dull, brittle and not sonorous. Metals are good conductors of heat and electricity, while non-metals are poor conductors. Metals have high melting points and high densities, whereas non-metals have low melting points and low densities. One similarity is that both are elements found on the Periodic Table.
