3 · Metals vs non-metals

🔨 METALS (left side)
Shiny · strong · bend (malleable) · pull to wire (ductile) · ring when hit (sonorous) · carry heat and electricity · dense
💔 NON-METALS (top right)
Dull · brittle · poor conductors · light · low melting point · no ring
Between them: metalloids (B, Si, Ge) = semi-conductors
Memory trick: Group 1 metals want to GIVE, halogens want to GRAB, noble gases are happy as they are.
