2 · Extra facts for each group

Group 1 extras

🛢 Stored in oil: stops reaction with oxygen
🔪 Soft: cut with a knife
🏊 Li, Na and K float on water
🫧 Li slow fizz · Na melts to a ball · K lilac flame
💜 Universal indicator turns purple (alkali)
🧪 Metal + water → hydroxide + hydrogen
🌫 Tarnish: dull oxide layer (Li slow, K very quick)
🧪 Metal + oxygen → metal oxide (Na₂O)
🔮 Caesium: even more violent than potassium

Group 7 extras

🧂 “Halogen” means salt-former
🚫 Never found free in nature: too reactive
👯 Diatomic: pairs of atoms (Cl₂, Br₂)
☠ Toxic and smelly
🏊 Chlorine kills bacteria in water and pools
🧂 Halogen + metal → metal halide (salt)
🔁 Displacement: Cl > Br > I. A stronger halogen pushes out a weaker one
🧶 Iron wool test: reactivity falls down the group

Group 0 extras

🔒 Full outer shell of electrons
😴 Inert: they do not react
🎈 Helium: balloons
💡 Argon: light bulbs
🪧 Neon: signs
📍 Far right of the table

3 · Metals vs non-metals

🔨 METALS (left side)
Shiny · strong · bend (malleable) · pull to wire (ductile) · ring when hit (sonorous) · carry heat and electricity · dense
💔 NON-METALS (top right)
Dull · brittle · poor conductors · light · low melting point · no ring
Between them: metalloids (B, Si, Ge) = semi-conductors
Memory trick: Group 1 metals want to GIVE, halogens want to GRAB, noble gases are happy as they are.
