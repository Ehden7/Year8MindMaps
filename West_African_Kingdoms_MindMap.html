The extract gives the impression that Timbuktu was an open and important centre of learning. The word “welcomed” suggests scholars were encouraged to share knowledge. “Many lands” suggests it brought together ideas from different places.

“Explain why…?” (4 marks) · one PEEL paragraph

POne reason

One reason Timbuktu became an important centre of trade was its location. It connected routes across West Africa and the Sahara. This helped merchants bring goods into its markets. Therefore, its connections attracted trade and made the city rich.
